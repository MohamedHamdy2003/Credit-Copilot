# Synthetic Vehicle Credit Policy

Edition AUTO-2025 · Product AL-200 · Effective 1 March 2025

This fictional supplement was authored for the Credit Copilot test corpus. It applies only to AL-200 vehicle finance.

## CP-3.1 Confirmed employment

The applicant must be a confirmed salaried employee and must have completed probation.

## CP-3.2 Employment duration

The applicant must have completed at least 6 months in current employment on the application date.

## CP-3.3 Minimum income

Verified net monthly income must be at least EGP 12,000.

## CP-3.4 Minimum age

The applicant must be at least 21 years old on the application date.

## CP-3.5 Maturity age

The final instalment may fall on or before the applicant's 60th birthday. Recheck the final payment date before issuing an approved offer.

## CP-4.1 Debt burden ratio

Maximum debt burden ratio is 40% of verified net monthly income. Add the EGP cent-rounded new monthly instalment to existing monthly obligations. Apply the limit before rounding the displayed debt burden percentage.

## CP-4.2 Monthly payment

Calculate equal monthly instalments on the reducing balance using financed principal, the dated annual rate from the AL-200 pricing table, and the selected tenor. The financed principal is the verified vehicle price less the documented customer down payment.

## CP-4.3 Maximum eligible financing

Apply the debt burden, product amount, and maximum loan-to-value limits and round the maximum eligible amount down to the nearest EGP 1,000. If the documented purchase is unaffordable, refer it for a fresh assessment with documented revised funding. An automated reduced offer is unavailable for this product.

## CP-5 Salary evidence

A stamped employer salary certificate issued within the 60 calendar days preceding application, including the application date, is required. Refer missing, stale, contradictory, or unverifiable records.

## CP-6 Bureau evidence

A score below 620 requires human review. A facility in default prohibits approval.

## CP-8 Human referrals

Refer missing required evidence, including missing national ID or vehicle price, to a human reviewer. Refer bank employees to a human reviewer. Suspected tampering follows the synthetic procedure's Fraud Unit path.

## CP-11 Purpose

AL-200 supports a documented vehicle purchase only. Commercial and speculative purposes are prohibited.
