# Synthetic Vehicle Loan Procedures

Document PM-AL-200 · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 March 2025

This fictional supplement applies only to AL-200. It adopts the same human authority safeguards as the supplied procedures manual.

## PM-1 Roles

The Loan Officer prepares a case and has no approval authority. Human approval requires a Credit Officer with sufficient authority. A Credit Officer may approve up to EGP 250,000, a Senior Credit Officer up to EGP 500,000, and the Head of Credit up to EGP 1,000,000. Product limits continue to apply.

## PM-2 Independent approval

A reviewer may never approve a case they prepared. Record the independent approver, decision, amount, and written justification before issuing any offer. An assessment recommendation and a policy question cannot approve or issue an offer.

## PM-3 Issuing an offer

Only an authorised Human Credit Officer may issue an approved offer. Recheck the policy and maximum age at maturity before issue. Ineligible or unresolved cases require review and verified reassessment.

## PM-4 Human review

The Senior Credit Officer reviews referrals with an indicative target of two business days. A documented review does not bypass missing evidence or eligibility.

## PM-7 Suspected tampering

Refer suspected tampering to the Fraud Unit and record the referral. Suspend approval or issuance.

## PM-9 Written reasons

An applicant may request the written reason for a human rejection within 30 calendar days. Cite the applicable failed clause and recorded decision. A written reason does not change the decision.
