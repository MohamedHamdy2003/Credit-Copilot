# Synthetic Vehicle Loan Product Sheet

Document PS-AL-200 · Version 1.0 · Effective 1 March 2025

AL-200 is a fictional instalment loan authored solely for the Credit Copilot test corpus.

## PS-3 Amount and tenor

Financed principal must be between EGP 30,000 and EGP 750,000 inclusive, with a tenor of 12 to 60 months. Financed principal equals the vehicle price less the documented down payment.

## PS-4 Pricing

The annual reducing-balance rate comes from the dated synthetic vehicle pricing table PT-AL-200 for the tenor and salary transfer segment.

## PS-5 Repayment

Equal monthly instalments start one month after disbursement. Round monthly instalments to the nearest EGP cent.

## PS-6 Fees

The synthetic administrative fee is 1% of approved principal and is deducted at disbursement. The synthetic early-settlement fee is 2% of outstanding principal. The synthetic late fee is EGP 150 per missed instalment. Fees are not financed or included in debt burden.

## PS-7 Required documents

Collect the application, a national ID copy, a current stamped salary certificate, a bank-obtained credit bureau report, and a vehicle quotation showing the price with documented down payment. Refer missing or unverifiable vehicle funding evidence.

## PS-9 Minimum down payment

The documented down payment must be at least 25% of the verified vehicle price. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is 75%. A complete case below the minimum down payment is ineligible. Refer cases where the vehicle price or down payment is missing. Reassess any revised funding before issuing an offer.
