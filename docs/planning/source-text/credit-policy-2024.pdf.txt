CP-1. Purpose and scope
This policy governs the granting of unsecured personal loans to salaried individuals. The 
edition of this policy that applies to an application is the edition in force on the date the 
application is received. A later edition never applies retroactively to an application already 
received.
CP-2. Definitions
• Net monthly income: salary after tax, social insurance and mandatory deductions, as 
shown on the salary certificate.
• Existing monthly obligations: the total monthly instalments of all credit facilities 
reported in the applicant’s credit bureau report, including credit card minimum 
payments.
• Debt burden ratio (DBR): existing monthly obligations plus the instalment of the 
requested loan, divided by net monthly income.
• Age at maturity: the applicant’s age at the due date of the final instalment.

CP-4. Affordability
CP-4.1 Maximum debt burden ratio
The DBR of an approved application must not exceed 50% of net monthly income.
CP-4.2 Calculation of the instalment
The monthly instalment is calculated on a reducing balance: instalment = P x r / (1 - 
(1 + r)^-n), where P is the loan amount, r is the applicable annual rate divided by 12, and n 
is the tenor in months. The result is rounded to two decimal places, half up.
CP-4.3 Maximum eligible amount
Where the requested amount fails CP-4.1, the bank shall calculate the largest loan amount 
whose instalment keeps the DBR at the maximum permitted, and may offer that amount 
instead. The maximum eligible amount is rounded down to the nearest EGP 1,000.
CP-4.4 Order of assessment
Eligibility (CP-3) is assessed first. Affordability (CP-4) is assessed only for applicants who 
satisfy CP-3.
CP-5. Income verification
Net monthly income is evidenced by a salary certificate issued within the last 60 days and 
stamped by the employer. Where the certificate and the bureau report disagree on employment, 
the application is referred to a credit officer.
CP-6. Credit bureau
A bureau report must be obtained for every application. - Score 600 and above: proceed to 
affordability assessment. - Score below 600: refer to a credit officer. Such applications are not 
automatically declined. - Any facility reported as in default: decline.
CP-7. Amount, tenor and pricing
Amounts and tenors are limited by the product sheet in force. The interest rate is taken from 
the approved pricing table by tenor band. Deviations from published pricing require Credit 
Committee approval.
CP-8. Referral and decline
An application is referred (not decided by the system) where: evidence is missing or 
unreadable, documents contradict each other, the bureau score is below the minimum, or the 
applicant is an employee of the bank.
An application is declined where a mandatory criterion in CP-3 fails, the DBR exceeds CP-4.1 
and the applicant rejects the maximum eligible amount, or a facility is in default.

CP-9. Non-discrimination
Credit decisions shall not take into account the applicant’s gender, religion, marital status or 
nationality. These attributes may be collected for statistical reporting only and must not be 
used in assessment.
CP-10. Documentation of decisions
Every decision must record: the policy edition applied, the figures used, the source document 
for each figure, the rule results, and the identity of the approver.
CP-11. Prohibited purposes
Loans may not be granted for business or commercial purposes, the purchase of securities, or 
any purpose prohibited by the circulars in force.
CP-12. Exceptions
Any exception to this policy requires written approval from the Head of Consumer Credit and 
must be recorded in the credit file.
