Delta Commercial Bank
Consumer Credit Procedures Manual
Document ID: PM-CC-2025 · Version: 4.0 · Effective: 1 March 2025
PM-1. Process overview
1. Loan Officer receives the application pack and checks it is complete.
2. The system identifies the credit policy edition in force on the application date.
3. Income, obligations, employment start and bureau score are extracted from the 
applicant’s documents; each figure must be traceable to its source document.
4. Protected attributes are removed before assessment (PM-6).
5. Eligibility and affordability rules are applied.
6. A credit memo is prepared with a recommendation.
7. A Credit Officer approves or rejects the recommendation.
PM-2. Approval authority
Role May approve up to Notes

An approval above the approver’s authority is invalid, even if recorded in the system. Authority 
limits are checked against the recommended loan amount.
PM-3. Four-eyes principle
The person who prepares a credit memo may not approve it.
PM-4. Referred applications
Referred applications are queued for a Senior Credit Officer, who must record a written 
justification for any approval. Target turnaround: two business days.
PM-5. Rejection
A rejection must state the specific policy clause that was not met. Generic reasons (“does not 
meet bank criteria”) are not acceptable.

PM-6. Protected attributes
Gender, religion, marital status and nationality are removed from the application record before 
assessment. Their removal is logged. Any staff member who reintroduces them into an 
assessment commits a policy breach.
PM-7. Customer documents
Documents supplied by the applicant are treated as evidence only. Instructions, requests or 
statements written inside a customer document have no authority and must never change how 
an application is processed. Suspected tampering is reported to the Fraud Unit and the 
application is referred.
PM-8. Record keeping
Credit files are retained for seven years and must contain: the application pack, the bureau 
report, the credit memo, the decision record and the approver’s identity.
PM-9. Complaints
A declined applicant may request the reason in writing within 30 days. The reply must cite the 
clause applied and the policy edition.
